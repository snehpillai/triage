# Refund Policy

## 1. Eligibility Window

Refund requests must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the confirmed delivery date. The delivery date is defined as the date of the carrier's delivery confirmation scan, not the date the customer received the package. Requests submitted after 30 days are not eligible for review under standard policy.

## 2. Full Refund Conditions

A full refund is issued when:

- The item arrived visibly damaged or broken at delivery (photo documentation required at time of request)
- The wrong item was shipped — model, color, or size does not match the order confirmation
- The item was not delivered within 10 business days of the estimated delivery date and tracking shows no movement for 5 or more consecutive business days
- The carrier confirms the item is lost in transit

Full refunds are returned to the original payment method. Store credit is not issued in place of a full refund without the customer's explicit request.

## 3. Partial Refund Conditions

A partial refund may be issued when:

- The item was received in working condition but is missing one or more accessories explicitly listed in the product description. The refund amount equals the documented retail value of the missing accessory, not a percentage of the order total.
- The item functions but has minor cosmetic damage not present in the product photos (scratches, scuffs, dents). Refund range is 10–25% of the item price, assessed case by case based on documentation.
- Delivery arrived more than 5 business days after the estimated date but within the 10-business-day threshold for full refund eligibility.

Partial refunds are credited to the original payment method or as store credit at the customer's discretion.

## 4. Refund Denial Reasons

The following conditions result in automatic denial:

- Request submitted more than 30 calendar days after confirmed delivery
- Damage is determined to be customer-caused: drops, liquid exposure, unauthorized disassembly, or physical modification
- Item falls under non-returnable categories: perishable goods, personal care items used after opening, customized or personalized products, and hazardous materials
- Digital goods where download or activation has been confirmed (see Section 6)
- Final sale or clearance items without a documented defect (see Section 7)
- Customer changed their mind on a purchase that does not meet damage, error, or non-delivery criteria

## 5. Refund Timelines by Payment Method

**Credit card and debit card**
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of refund approval. After processing, the credit may take an additional 1–3 business days to appear on the customer's statement depending on the issuing bank. The total visible timeline is typically 6–10 business days from approval.

**PayPal**
Refunds to PayPal balances are processed within 3–5 business days of approval. If the original PayPal transaction was funded by a linked credit or debit card, the credit card timeline above applies instead of the PayPal balance timeline.

**Bank transfer (ACH / direct debit)**
Refunds to bank accounts via ACH transfer are processed within 10–14 business days of approval. This timeline reflects ACH network processing requirements and cannot be expedited regardless of the circumstances.

**Store credit**
Store credit refunds are applied to the account immediately upon approval. No banking processing time applies. Store credit does not expire and can be applied to any future order.

## 6. Digital Goods Exception

Digital goods — including software licenses, downloadable files, subscription activations, in-app purchases, and access codes — are not eligible for refund once the item has been downloaded, activated, or accessed.

Refunds on digital goods are only considered when the item is technically defective and cannot function for its intended purpose. To qualify:

- The customer must document the defect with screenshots, error logs, or a reproducible description that can be verified by the technical support team
- The defect must be confirmed by technical support as a product issue, not a device or connectivity issue on the customer's side
- The refund request must be submitted within 14 days of purchase

## 7. Sale and Clearance Items

**Sale items** — items discounted during a promotional event but not explicitly marked final sale — are eligible for refund under the standard 30-day policy. The refund amount is the amount the customer paid, not the original pre-discount price.

**Final sale items** — items explicitly marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase — are not eligible for refunds or exchanges unless the item arrives defective or the wrong item was shipped. If either condition is met, full refund applies.

**Clearance items** — items from discontinued inventory, marked as clearance — are eligible for store credit only, not cash or card refunds, unless the item arrives defective or is the wrong item. Defective clearance items are eligible for a full refund to the original payment method.

## 8. How to Request a Refund

1. Submit a refund request through the account portal or by contacting support directly. Include the order number, the specific item being returned, and the reason for the refund. Photo documentation should be attached at this step for damage claims — submitting it later extends processing time.
2. A Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number will be issued within 1 business day of request submission. Packages returned without a valid RMA number will not be processed and will be returned to the sender at the sender's expense.
3. Ship the item back using the prepaid return label provided with the RMA (for eligible damage and error claims) or at the customer's expense (for change-of-mind returns where free return eligibility does not apply). The item must be shipped within 10 business days of RMA issuance. RMAs expire after 10 business days.
4. Once the returned item is received and inspected, a confirmation email is sent and the refund is initiated. Physical inspection is completed within 2 business days of receipt at the warehouse.

## 9. Dispute Process

If a refund request is denied and the customer believes the denial was made in error:

1. The customer submits a dispute by replying to the denial notification email with additional documentation supporting their claim.
2. A senior support review is completed within 72 business hours of dispute submission. The customer is notified of the outcome by email.
3. If the dispute is upheld, the refund is processed under the standard payment method timelines in Section 5.
4. If the dispute is denied a second time, the customer may request escalation to the customer experience team. This review takes 5–7 business days and constitutes the final internal decision.

Filing a chargeback with the payment provider while an internal dispute is open will result in the internal dispute being closed immediately. The two processes cannot run concurrently.
